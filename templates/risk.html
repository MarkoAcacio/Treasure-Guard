How the score works

  1. Each kind of check gives the payment a score from 0 to 100: the rules (double charges, limits, spending jumps), the statistics (Z-score and IQR) and the AI models.
  2. The risk starts at the highest of those.
  3. If two kinds agree, it goes up by 15. If all three agree, by 25. It never goes above 100.

75 and up is High, 50 to 74 Medium, below 50 Low. Flags you marked OK don't count, so a payment drops off this list once it checks out.
